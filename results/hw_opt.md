## Reading (hand-written from the tables above; not regenerated by the script)

- **Accuracy-vs-CX: yes, prune + refine moves the frontier.** With the exact reference, every start reaches
  100% of held-out geometries within 1.6 mHa at far fewer CX than any zero-evaluation operating point that
  keeps VAL within target: H4 test_fresh ~668 CX (sft/krr start) vs 923 for KRR-prefix/pruned (VAL-chosen
  level 17 = full sequence; truncated ADAPT's VAL point: 831 CX, and only 15/20 runs are that long);
  LiH6 test_fresh 162 CX vs 223-290 (and those one-shot points reach only 71% on test_fresh).
  At the same per-geometry CX, KRR prefixes are within target on only 18-60% and truncated ADAPT on 41-88%.
- **Cost: it is not one-shot.** H4 spends ~2,400-2,900 energy evaluations per geometry on pruning +
  refinement, i.e. 2.5-3x what fresh ADAPT needs to build its whole circuit (949); LiH6 ~250-310
  (fresh ADAPT: 249). The gain is a CX gain bought with evaluations, not a free improvement.
- **Reference-free pruning (blind, tol chosen on VAL)** gives nearly the same CX but loses accuracy on H4
  (90-95% within target), because accumulated increases plus a non-zero start error can cross 1.6 mHa.
- The SFT and KRR starts end at identical circuits (same canonical sequence, refinement reaches the same
  optimum), so the starting generator does not matter once evaluations are allowed.
- Level-3 transpilation (`cx_l3` in the jsonl) removes a further ~7-9% CX for any circuit; it does not change
  the ranking.
