How to read these tables

test_fresh is the result: geometries drawn with a fixed seed after development finished and scored once with frozen checkpoints. test is the original grid split (interpolation points, a held-out interval and extrapolation), which was seen while the label recipe and training were tuned; it is kept for reference. Errors are |E − EFCI| in mHa against exact diagonalisation of the same active-space Hamiltonian; the ≤ columns give the share of geometries within 1.6, 5 and 10 mHa. Quantum evals is the mean number of energy evaluations plus ADAPT pool-gradient evaluations spent per geometry to propose the circuit. CX is the Qiskit CX-basis count. Rows in blue are one-shot transformer generators. The method id under each name is the result file: results_fresh/<system>/<id>.jsonl and results/<system>/<id>.jsonl.
